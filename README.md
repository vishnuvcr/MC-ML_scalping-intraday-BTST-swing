# MC / ML for Scalping, Intraday, BTST & Swing

Research project studying whether Monte Carlo methods can improve the statistical validation and/or decision support of stock-trading systems across scalping, intraday, BTST and swing horizons.

## Current status
**Phase 5 robustness design complete; empirical phases remain blocked by validated market-data access.**

The research protocol, literature review, cost model, Monte Carlo methods, statistical plan and robustness plan are now documented. No stock-level profitability result has been fabricated.

## Conclusion so far
Monte Carlo has a defensible role as a resampling, uncertainty, null-testing and risk-analysis layer. It is not evidence of guaranteed trading alpha. Robust profitability requires net out-of-sample market data, multiple-testing controls and realistic execution costs.
