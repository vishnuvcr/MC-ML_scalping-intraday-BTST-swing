# MC / ML for Scalping, Intraday, BTST & Swing

Research project studying whether Monte Carlo methods can improve the statistical validation and/or decision support of stock-trading systems across scalping, intraday, BTST and swing horizons.

## Final research status
**Phase 6 complete as a data-gated research report (20 September 2026).**

The finite research program has completed the protocol, literature review, data/cost framework, Monte Carlo methods, statistical design, robustness design and manuscript. The empirical profitability phases stop because validated/licensed market data for the required horizons were not available in the repository.

## Conclusion
Monte Carlo is supported as a validation, resampling, uncertainty, null-testing and risk-analysis layer. The study does **not** establish that Monte Carlo itself creates profitable stock-trading alpha. No stock-level profitability result is claimed without the missing market-data experiment.

## Key finding from cost model
Under the provisional cost configuration, a ₹100,000 buy + ₹100,000 sell costs about ₹82.68 intraday before spread/slippage and about ₹182.68 with 2 bps spread + 3 bps slippage per leg. Delivery/BTST/swing is about ₹269.68 before those execution frictions. These are arithmetic illustrations, not live brokerage quotes.
